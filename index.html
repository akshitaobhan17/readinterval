The idea

We forgot how to be bored.

Every spare minute became another scroll, another notification, another thing asking for our attention.

So we made the paper we wanted to find on a coffee table, in a bookshop or tucked into a café corner. Games, puzzles, odd little facts and absolutely nothing that needs updating.
